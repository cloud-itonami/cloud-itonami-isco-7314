# Business Model: Pottery Workshop Scheduling and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7314`
- ISCO-08: `7314`
- Occupation: Potters and Related Workers
- Social impact: worker-safety, product-quality, craft-continuity

## Customer

- pottery workshop operators / ceramics studio owners
- independent potters crews / potters cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- clay-batch/glaze-materials-usage/kiln-load progress-record logging
- clay/glaze-materials supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to workshop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a firing/glazing-execution decision
  (e.g. deciding to proceed with a specific kiln firing), ever
- no override of a workshop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (burn exposure, glaze-chemical/dust
  exposure, kiln condition) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
